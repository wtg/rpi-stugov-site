Who runs club funding

The Union Constitution gives the Executive Board the power to hand out Union money (Art. VII §2), but not the application steps. We are confirming whether requests go through the Club Financial Advisors, Union staff, or another system. {% include 'lab/front-door/_draft.html' with why="Union staff process" %}

How this site works

This site keeps one copy of each fact. Files live in Box. Each body keeps its meeting records its own way: the Senate in its Notion, the Executive Board on this site, and the other bodies in their Box folders. This site keeps the roster for every body and its own explanations.
