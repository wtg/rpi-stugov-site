Contingency funds

Under the Union Constitution, the Executive Board prepares and approves the Union budget and hands out Union money. The budget and the records of all extra spending and allocations are public documents. {% include 'lab/the-record/_cite.html' with art='Art. VII §2' %}

No Union money may be spent without an Executive Board requisition (a formal spending approval), and RPI's Treasurer gets a copy of every transaction. {% include 'lab/the-record/_cite.html' with art='Art. XIV; Art. VI' %}
