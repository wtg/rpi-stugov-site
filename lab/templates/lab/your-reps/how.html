Speak at a meeting

You don't have to be on the Senate or the Executive Board to be heard. Agendas include an Invitation to Speak at the start and end of the meeting, and a Press & Visitor Q/A. Come to a meeting and use them. Check the meeting list above for the time and place. {% include "lab/your-reps/_draft.html" with why="Senate bylaws or rules" %}

Elections

When

General elections are in the spring. First-year Senators and the first-year Class Council are elected in the fall. Results come out by the end of the semester and winners take office within a week. {% include "lab/your-reps/_cite.html" with art="Art. VIII §1–§3, §12" %}

Who can vote and run

Any Union member at the time of the election; voting is by secret ballot. Candidates must meet RPI's eligibility rules and any Senate rules. Grand Marshal, President of the Union and Judicial Board Chairperson need one full semester at RPI. {% include "lab/your-reps/_cite.html" with art="Art. IV §6; Art. VIII §4, §13" %}

How winners are chosen

Grand Marshal and President of the Union need at least 40% of the vote. Most other seats go to whoever gets the most votes. The three FSL seats are spread across chapter groupings: the top vote-getter wins, and the next two seats go to the top candidates from different groupings (or write-ins). Ties mean a new election between the tied candidates. {% include "lab/your-reps/_cite.html" with art="Art. VIII §5, §8, §10, §11" %}

How this site works

This site keeps one copy of each fact. It keeps the roster for every body and its own explanations. Files live in Box. Each body keeps its meeting records its own way: the Senate in its Notion, the Executive Board on this site, and the other bodies as files in Box.

What members control

Name, seat and term are always public, because they're on public agendas. Each member decides on this site whether to publish a photo, bio, pronouns, major and office hours. Unticking 'Public profile' removes them from this site at once. Contact always goes to the seat's role inbox.

At the start of a new term

Set the new term as current on this site. Every seat on this site then shows open until new assignments are entered. Nobody clears last year's names by hand. The Senate's Notion copy updates itself.
